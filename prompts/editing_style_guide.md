# Editing Style Guide

## Account Feel

Not ESPN. Not SportsCenter. Not a highlight dump.

The account should feel:
- cinematic
- emotional
- atmospheric
- mythological
- built for Americans discovering football culture

## Clip Structure

### 0–2 seconds
Hard hook.

Examples:
- American sports do not feel like this.
- The World Cup changes entire countries.
- Football atmosphere is psychologically insane.
- America is not ready for this sport.

### 2–8 seconds
Build tension.

Use:
- crowd buildup
- tunnel shots
- commentary layering
- slowed reaction shots
- faces before action

### 8–18 seconds
Payoff.

Use:
- goal
- scream
- eruption
- collapse
- shock
- celebration

### Final 2 seconds
Aftermath.

Use:
- crying fan
- silence
- player stare
- stunned crowd
- flag in crowd
