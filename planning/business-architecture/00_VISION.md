# Vision

## Purpose

This repository should evolve from a World Cup-focused implementation into an agency-first media operations business for organizations that create live or long-form media.

The software should support the business. It should not become a broad platform before repeated client needs prove which workflows are worth productizing.

## Company Naming

The current names are too narrow for the future direction:

- World Cup Clipping Pipeline is tied to one deployment.
- Stadium Signal is tied to football and sports storytelling.

The business needs a broader company name before implementation work begins. Until then, these documents use "the company" and "the operating system" instead of a final brand name.

The name should avoid being tied to:

- Sports.
- Clipping.
- AI.
- Video only.

The name should be broad enough to support media operations across sports, creators, podcasts, conferences, events, archives, and future content formats.

## Current Starting Point

The existing repository already supports parts of a managed media operations workflow:

- Local video recording and processing scripts.
- Transcription through local Whisper or OpenAI.
- Prompt-based content analysis through local or hosted models.
- CSV and JSON manifests.
- FFmpeg-based clip exports.
- A local static review dashboard.
- A strong football case study built around World Cup-style storytelling.

It does not yet support a general commercial service without manual setup and source-code changes.

## Business Direction

The business should start as a managed service:

- We configure and operate workflows for clients.
- Clients provide content access, brand direction, and approval feedback.
- The agency delivers repeatable content outputs.
- Software is introduced where it reduces repeated manual work.

The long-term product path is an operations platform, not necessarily a pure SaaS product.

## What We Are Building Toward

A reusable media operations system that can serve:

- Sports leagues and teams.
- Tournaments and events.
- Esports organizations.
- Streamers and creators.
- Podcasts and interview shows.
- Conferences and business events.
- Long-form media publishers.

The system should manage:

- Organizations.
- Projects.
- Content sources.
- Brand systems.
- Workflow templates.
- Jobs.
- Outputs.
- Assets.
- Review and approval.
- Delivery and reporting.

## What We Are Not Building Yet

Do not build these until client demand proves the need:

- Self-serve SaaS onboarding.
- Billing automation.
- Public dashboard.
- Full multi-tenancy.
- Direct social publishing.
- Marketplace integrations.
- Large-scale distributed processing.

## Strategic Position

Automated clipping alone is becoming common. The stronger opportunity is reliable media operations:

- Consistent turnaround.
- Good editorial judgment.
- Repeatable production workflows.
- Brand-safe outputs.
- Human review where needed.
- Clear provenance from source to final asset.

The World Cup implementation should become the first reference deployment, not the product boundary.

## Vision Statement

The company helps organizations transform long-form and live media into reusable content through repeatable workflows.

This vision intentionally does not depend on a specific sport, content format, model provider, or output type.
